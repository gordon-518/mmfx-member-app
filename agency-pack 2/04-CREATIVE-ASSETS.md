## 2. Naming convention — please continue it

```
{FEATURE}-{TYPE}{n}-{descriptor}.{ext}
```

- **FEATURE:** `AITA` (AI Trading Assistant) · `KYS` · `COURSE` · `FUND` · `DAILY` ·
  `IND` · `STRAT` · `SIGNALS` · `CLASSES` · `EBOOK` · `BOTS`
- **TYPE:** `I` = static image · `V` = video
- **n:** sequence number · **descriptor:** the hook in one word

**Example:** `AITA-I6-scorecard.png`

**Keep the creative filename aligned with the `cid` in the ad URL** (see
`03-LANDING-PAGES-AND-TRACKING.md` §3) so a creative can be traced from file →
ad → signup → funded member. E.g. file `AITA-I6-scorecard.png` →
`cid=CRT-0917-AITA-SCORE-I6`.

## 3. Brand direction

**Feel:** warm-premium, calm, editorial. Closer to a fintech product page than a
trading-guru ad.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Accent** | Signal orange `#ff5a1f` — used sparingly, for emphasis only |
| **Ink / text** | Near-black warm `#1a1714` |
| **Surfaces** | Warm off-white `#fbfaf8`, white cards, soft peach `#ffece2` |
| **Type** | Bricolage Grotesque (headlines) · Hanken Grotesk (body) |
| **Imagery** | Real product UI, charts, clean device mock-ups |

**Visual don'ts:** ❌ cars/watches/cash/jets ❌ neon "trading room" clichés
❌ stock-photo men in suits pointing at screens ❌ green candle explosions
❌ cluttered, hype-heavy layouts

**Look at `AITA-I4` / `AITA-I5` first** — they're the clearest expression of
where the brand is going.

## 5. Creative angles that fit the product

Each maps to a landing page — **match the ad promise to the LP**.

**AI Trading Assistant** → `/lp/ai-trading-assistant`
1. *"You don't have a strategy problem. You have a nobody's-keeping-score problem."*

**Know Your Style** → `/lp/know-your-style`
5. *"Fourteen questions. Then you'll know why your style keeps failing."*
6. *"You're copying someone else's style — and blaming yourself."*

**Daily Analysis** → `/lp/daily-analysis`
7. *"Every session, the gold read — before you risk a thing."*
8. Consistency as proof: a read published day after day

**Course** → `/lp/mm-system`
9. *"19 lessons. Basic to institutional."* — for the overwhelmed beginner

⚠️ Every one of these must still pass `05-COMPLIANCE.md`. Note that none promise
an outcome — they promise **clarity, diagnosis and a system**. Keep new angles in
that register.
