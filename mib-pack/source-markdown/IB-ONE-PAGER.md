# Your own trading desk. Built, branded and run for you.

**For introducing brokers partnered with Dupoin Markets**

---

## The problem

You have an audience. You have a referral link. What you don't have is a product.

So your traders open an account, trade for a few weeks, and drift. Not because your
introduction was bad — because there was nothing to come back to. No tools, no structure,
nothing that makes them better. Accounts go quiet, and quiet accounts pay nobody.

---

## What you get

**A complete trading desk under your own brand, on your own domain.**

Your traders sign up on your site, see your logo, and get:

**A professional indicator suite** — ten custom TradingView indicators and two backtestable
strategies, delivered to their TradingView account automatically the moment they enter
their username. No codes to send, no support tickets for you to answer.

**An AI trading assistant** — this is the one that changes behaviour. It connects
**read-only** to their live trading account, imports every trade automatically, and shows
them what they're actually doing: the habits costing them money in dollars, a discipline
score, and an AI coach that reviews their trading against goals they set themselves. It
cannot trade, it cannot withdraw, and their password is never stored.

**A research desk** — an interactive bot that answers what's driving gold right now and
emails them a written macro thesis.

**A trader profiling quiz, an economic calendar, and filtered market news** across nineteen
instruments.

**A short foundation course**, so your desk is never empty on day one.

**And your own content, if you have it.** Upload your course, your eBooks, your analysis,
your signals, your class schedule — it appears on your desk, under your brand. If you don't
have content, the tools stand on their own.

## What you bring

Your audience. Your brand. Your market and your language. First-line contact with your own
traders.

## Why this works

A trader with a referral link has no reason to log in tomorrow. A trader with a desk — with
their performance in front of them, their leaks named, their charts equipped, and a coach
reviewing their decisions — has a reason to log in every day.

Traders who stay engaged stay funded. Traders who stay funded keep trading.

We're paid on volume, the same way you are — so we only do well when your traders stay
active. Our incentives and yours point in the same direction.
