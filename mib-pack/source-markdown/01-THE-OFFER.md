# 01 · The Offer

## Positioning

**MMFX is infrastructure for forex IBs.**

Not an agency on retainer, and not a franchise. MMFX supplies the platform, the product
suite and the funnel that turn an IB with an audience into an IB with something to sell —
under the IB's own brand, on the IB's own domain.

The closest analogue outside this industry is Shopify. Shopify does not sell products and
does not compete with its merchants for brand equity; it makes it possible for them to
have a store at all. MMFX proposes the same relationship with Dupoin's IB network.

This matters commercially: **MMFX does not compete with Dupoin's IBs for their audience or
their brand.** Every trader acquired stays visibly the IB's trader, on the IB's desk, and
funds a Dupoin account. MMFX is invisible to the end trader by design.

---

## How value is actually created

Every service in this pack is judged against one equation:

```
Volume = funded accounts  x  lots per account per month  x  months the account stays alive
         |__ lead gen __|    |___ product / engagement ___|  |_______ retention _______|
```

**Most IB support programmes only address the first term.** Referral links, banner sets,
a landing page template. They increase how many accounts open. They do nothing about what
happens next, which is where the volume actually is.

**The third term is where MMFX is different.** An IB with a desk gives their trader a
reason to return daily: their trades imported automatically, their behavioural leaks
quantified in dollars, a discipline score that moves, a coach that reviews their decisions
against their own stated goals, indicators live on their charts, a calendar telling them
what is about to move gold. That trader stays funded longer and trades more. A trader with
a referral link and nothing else churns.

**This is the entire argument for an override rather than a flat fee.** MMFX is paid on
volume because MMFX's product is what produces the volume — not just the introduction.

## The three tiers

### Tier 1 — Equip

**No fee, no subscription. The default.**

The complete white-labelled desk: the full tool suite, the short foundation course, the
signup-to-funded funnel, lifecycle email, analytics, and the studio for uploading their own
content. Their brand, their domain, their traders.

The IB writes no cheque — no setup fee, no monthly fee. MMFX is compensated out of the
per-lot pool, so the arrangement is funded by volume rather than by the IB. This should be
close to frictionless to accept: an IB with an audience and no product gets one without a
cash outlay, and MMFX only earns if their traders actually trade.

### Tier 2 — Equip + Capture

**Tier 1 plus the acquisition layer.**

Their own landing pages, conversion tracking with a creative-ID convention that ties spend
to funded accounts, lifecycle email sequences, and a lead dashboard. For IBs who are
already driving traffic and are losing it through a weak capture layer.

Small per-IB build. Suits IBs with an existing audience and no funnel.

### Tier 3 — Full agency

**Tier 2 plus MMFX runs the paid media.**

Meta and short-form campaign build and management, the full creative production line, geo
tiering, and creative refresh on a cadence. This is a genuine agency service and carries
genuine cost.

**Ad spend is funded by the IB or co-funded by Dupoin — never by MMFX.** The override is
paid after volume is generated; advertising is paid weeks before. See `05-COMMERCIAL`,
condition 2. This is the tier where the arrangement either compounds or bleeds, and it
should be entered deliberately, with a small number of IBs, once Tier 1 has proven out.
