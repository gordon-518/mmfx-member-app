# 05 · Commercial

## The ask

| Item | MMFX's position |
|---|---|
| **Override** | A per-lot override on all volume generated by IBs under the MMFX MIB, rate to be set by Dupoin. Tiered by service level if Dupoin prefers |
| **Data access** | Sub-IB-level volume reporting on a defined refresh cadence — see condition 1 |
| **Term** | A term long enough to justify the platform investment. MMFX builds before it earns |
| **Exclusivity** | Open to discussion in both directions — see open questions |
| **Trader portability** | To be settled in writing at the outset — see below |
| **Ad spend** | Funded by the IB or co-funded by Dupoin, never by MMFX — see condition 2 |

---

## Trader portability

If the arrangement ends, what happens to the traders MMFX acquired, onboarded and retained?

MMFX will have built the desk, run the acquisition, carried the support and kept the
accounts alive. Those traders will sit in Dupoin accounts under IBs Dupoin introduced.
Without an agreed position, this becomes a dispute at exactly the moment when goodwill is
lowest.

MMFX is not asking for a particular outcome here. It is asking that the outcome be written
down before either party has an incentive to argue about it.

---

## The three conditions

These are not preferences. Each one, if unmet, breaks the model in a specific and
predictable way.

### Condition 1 — Sub-IB-level volume reporting

**What is needed.** A regular data feed identifying, for every trading account under the
MMFX MIB:

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Sub-IB identifier | Attribute every trader to the IB who introduced them |
| Trading account number | Match to the platform member — the mechanism for this is already live |
| Account open date | Measure time-to-fund and cohort behaviour |
| Deposits and current balance | Verify funding and detect at-risk accounts |
| **Lots traded, per period** | **The single most important field.** It is what MMFX is paid on and what every optimisation decision depends on |
| Last trade date / account status | Measure survival, which is the core claim in `01` |

Weekly is workable. Daily is better. File export is acceptable; an API is preferable.

**Why it is non-negotiable.** Without it MMFX cannot attribute a funded account to an IB,
cannot tell a productive IB from an unproductive one, cannot optimise advertising toward
accounts that actually trade, cannot invoice against a verifiable number, and cannot prove
that any of this worked. Every commercial claim in this pack becomes unfalsifiable.

**How close this already is.** MMFX already ingests Dupoin's referred-account export,
parses it against a configurable column mapping, and matches trading account numbers to
platform members. That pipeline is live. The gap is two fields in the data — sub-IB and
lots — not a system that needs building.

**Also requested: Dupoin's baseline figures.** Average account survival, attrition and
lots per account per month across the existing IB book. Without a baseline, the retention
argument in `01` can be asserted but never demonstrated. With it, the pilot produces a
measured comparison — which is as useful to Dupoin as it is to MMFX.

### Condition 2 — Ad spend is not funded by MMFX

The override is paid after volume is generated. Advertising is paid weeks before it. Any
arrangement where MMFX funds media against a future override transfers the entire working
capital risk to MMFX for revenue it does not control.

Where MMFX runs paid media for an IB under Tier 3, that spend comes from the IB or is
co-funded by Dupoin. MMFX supplies the strategy, the creative, the build and the
management.

### Condition 3 — A minimum-volume threshold and a dormancy clause

Every live desk carries a monthly infrastructure cost from the day it launches: live
account connections billed per account, market data, email, hosting, database. An IB who
signs up, takes a branded desk and produces nothing is a permanent cost centre.

Every IB agreement therefore needs a minimum-volume threshold and a clear dormancy
position — a desk that produces nothing for a defined period is archived and can be
reactivated when the IB is ready.

This protects Dupoin as much as MMFX. A network of dormant branded desks is a liability
for both parties.

## Open questions for Dupoin

1. **Exclusivity.** Does Dupoin expect exclusivity from MMFX, and on what terms?
2. **Contracting.** Who is the counterparty on the IB agreement — MMFX contracting the IB
   directly, or Dupoin contracting the IB with MMFX as service provider? This determines
   where the content policy and compliance obligations in `07` are enforced.
3. **Market exclusivity between IBs.** Where two IBs under the MIB target the same market
   and language, is any territory or channel separation offered? Worth settling before it
   becomes a complaint.
4. **Override structure.** Flat per lot, or tiered by service level and volume?
