# 07 · Compliance

> MMFX operates in a regulated space — retail forex and CFD education, and introducing-broker
> referral — and advertises on platforms that treat financial services as a restricted
> category. In a multi-tenant arrangement these risks multiply: thirty IBs under one MIB can
> become thirty independent compliance problems unless one rule set is enforced across all
> of them.
>
> This document sets out that rule set and where liability sits.

---

## 1 · Where liability sits

Anything an IB publishes on their desk renders on a platform MMFX operates, on a domain
MMFX provisions, to traders funnelled into a Dupoin account under the MMFX MIB. A careless
claim by one IB is not contained to that IB.

**The position MMFX proposes:**

| Party | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| **The IB** | Compliance within their own jurisdiction; the accuracy and legality of content they upload; their own marketing claims |
| **MMFX** | The platform, the rule set, review before publication, enforcement and takedown; all creative MMFX itself produces |
| **Dupoin** | Broker-side regulatory requirements; confirming which markets are eligible |

**Enforcement mechanism.** A content policy annexed to every IB agreement, a review step
before anything an IB uploads goes live, and a takedown right MMFX can exercise
unilaterally. At pilot scale review is manual and entirely feasible. Before scaling beyond
roughly ten tenants it must become a defined workflow — that is a condition of Phase 3, not
an afterthought.

## 2 · Platform advertising risk

Forex and CFD-adjacent advertising sits under restricted-category financial services
policy on the major ad platforms. Some markets require prior written permission or
advertiser authorisation; some restrict the category outright.

Before any campaign is built for any tenant:

1. Confirm the ad account is in good standing and eligible for financial education and
   trading products in each target market
2. Confirm whether the market requires advertiser verification
3. Confirm no market-specific restriction rules the geo out
4. Confirm the positioning holds — this is **education and tools, never advice**, and the
   creative must make that obvious

Policy changes frequently and must be verified against current published policy rather
than this document.

**Multi-tenant note.** One ad account serving multiple IBs means one restriction takes
every tenant down with it. This is why the ad-account and Business Manager structure is a
Phase 3 prerequisite before Tier 3 scales, and not something to improvise.

## 3 · Never say, imply or show

Applies to MMFX and to every IB on the platform, without exception.

**Earnings and results — the hardest rule**
- Any profit, income or return: guaranteed, typical, projected or implied
- "Make $X a day/week/month", "replace your salary", "passive income"
- Profit screenshots — account balances, P&L, broker statements, win streaks
- Win rates or accuracy claims
- Testimonials referencing money made
- "Risk-free", "guaranteed", "can't lose"
- Backtest or historical performance figures

**Advice and outcome**
- Anything reading as financial advice or a personal recommendation
- "We'll make you profitable", "turn your account around"
- Claiming any tool predicts the market
- Suggesting trading is easy, quick or low-risk

**Pressure and imagery**
- Urgency pushing someone to deposit
- Wealth-flex imagery — cars, watches, cash, jets
- Framing a broker deposit as a payment to the IB or to MMFX. It is the trader's own
  capital in their own account, and it must always be described that way
- Targeting, or implying targeting of, vulnerable or financially distressed people

---

## 4 · Safe and true

- The platform provides **education, analysis and tools — not advice**
- The trial is genuinely free, no card required
- The AI Trading Assistant is **read-only**: it cannot trade, cannot withdraw, and the
  account password is never stored. Lead with this
- Specific factual product claims are fine: ten indicators, nineteen instruments,
  published every session
- Trading carries risk of loss; include risk messaging where required
- Outcomes depend entirely on the trader's own decisions
- Past performance never indicates future results

## 5 · The rewrite pattern

| Unsafe | Safe |
|---|---|
| "Stop losing money" | "See where your money is leaking" |
| "Make consistent profits" | "Build a consistent process" |
| "Our signals are 80% accurate" | "Trade ideas from the desk, with the reasoning" |
| "This indicator predicts reversals" | "This indicator highlights potential reversal zones" |
| "Turn your trading around" | "See what your account is actually doing" |
| "Trade like a pro and earn like one" | "Learn the system a professional actually uses" |
| "Join 10,000 profitable traders" | "Join traders learning the system" |

**The pattern: describe what the product does, never what the trader will earn.**

---

## 6 · Product-specific cautions

| Product | Watch for |
|---|---|
| **Signals** (IB-uploaded) | The most sensitive surface on the platform. Never quote accuracy, win rate, pips or profit. Signals are trade ideas to evaluate, never advice and never an income source |
| **Strategies** | Backtestable, but backtest returns are never published and future performance is never implied |
| **Indicators** | Analysis tools that highlight conditions. They do not predict |
| **AI Trading Assistant** | Lead with read-only, cannot trade, password never stored. It analyses; it does not advise or execute |
| **Courses** | Teach a method. They do not promise an outcome |
| **The broker offer** | The deposit is the trader's own capital in their own name. Never "pay us". Disclose the introducing-broker relationship where required |

---

## 7 · Member content

Any testimonial, review, screenshot or named trader requires written consent. No trader's
results may be presented as typical or expected. No aggregate statistics — member counts,
success rates — without confirmation that the figure is both accurate and safe to publish.

---

## 8 · Sign-off

Everything an IB uploads is reviewed before it publishes. The fastest path through review:

1. Check it against §3
2. Run it through the §5 rewrite pattern
3. Ask: does this promise an outcome, or describe a product? Only the second passes
