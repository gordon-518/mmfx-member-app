# 01 · Business Brief

## What Market Makers FX is

MMFX trains and equips retail **gold (XAU/USD) and forex traders**. Members get
a complete trading desk in one app: the daily market read, proprietary charting
tools, a full course, live teaching, and — as of Aug 2026 — an AI assistant that
analyses their actual trading account.

The founder and face of the brand is **Don**. All member-facing and ad copy
refers to Don, never any other name.

- **Member app:** `app.marketmakersfx.net`
- **Marketing site:** `marketmakersfx.net`
- **Community:** Telegram

## How the business makes money

**There is no subscription.** MMFX is an **introducing broker (IB)**.

A member keeps full access by **funding a trading account (US$500) with a
partner broker** through MMFX. That money is **the member's own trading capital,
in an account in their own name** — it is *not* a payment to MMFX. MMFX is paid
rebates by the broker based on the member's trading volume.

**This is the single most powerful thing in the offer, and it must be framed
correctly:**

✅ *"The desk is free — you just need a funded account to trade with."*
❌ *"Pay $500 for access."* (false, and a compliance problem)

### Regional offer differences — important for targeting

| Market | Offer |
|---|---|
| **Most of the world** | Fund a partner broker account → full access (money stays theirs) |
| **Malaysia / Indonesia** | Same, different partner broker |
| **US / UK** | ⚠️ **No partner broker available** — a one-time lifetime membership instead, arranged directly |

**Implication:** the standard "free desk, fund your own account" creative **does
not apply to US/UK**. Either exclude those markets or run a separate campaign
with different messaging. **Confirm with Don before targeting them.**

*(Commercial detail — broker names, rebate rates, membership pricing — is
internal. Ask Don if a campaign genuinely needs it; it must never appear in
public creative.)*

## The funnel

```
 Meta ad
   → Landing page  (marketmakersfx.net/lp/{feature})
   → Free signup   (email + password, or one-tap Google)
   → 7-DAY full-access trial — everything unlocked, no card
   → Trial ends → /upgrade (automatically routed by region)
   → Member funds a broker account
   → Admin verifies the deposit  →  Full access continues
```

**Conversion stages we can measure:** signup (trial start) → funded member.
See `03-LANDING-PAGES-AND-TRACKING.md` for the exact events.

**Why the funnel converts:** the member experiences the entire product free for
7 days. The upgrade isn't "buy something" — it's *"keep what you're already
using"*, and it costs them nothing in fees.

## Who the customer is

**Core audience:** retail traders trading their own money, focused on gold and
forex.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Geography** | Heavily **Southeast Asia** — Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia. Meaningful tail in Africa (Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya), South Asia, Middle East, and Europe |
| **Age/profile** | Predominantly men 25–45, self-taught, trading alongside a job or business |
| **Experience** | Mixed — from a few months in to several years. Most have *some* knowledge and *no* consistency |
| **Language** | English (the app is English-only) |

### What they're feeling (use this for angles)

- **"I keep giving it back."** Wins a week, loses it the next. Inconsistency is
  the number-one pain.
- **"I've blown accounts before."** Often more than once. There's shame in this
   — speak to it carefully and without mockery.
- **"I've bought courses that were rubbish."** High scepticism. Hype triggers
  their defences; plain speech disarms them.
- **"I don't know what I'm doing wrong."** They can't diagnose themselves —
  which is precisely what the AI Trading Assistant addresses.
- **"Too much information, no system."** Drowning in YouTube and signal groups.

### What they respond to
- Specificity over hype ("19 lessons", "10 indicators", "every session")
- Proof of real work — a daily analysis published day after day
- Being treated as an adult, not a mark
- **Diagnosis** — being shown what's actually wrong with their trading

### What repels them
- Lambos, watches, cash, "financial freedom" imagery
- Guru energy and guaranteed-profit language
- Anything that smells like the last course that burned them

## Competitive context

Most competitors are **signal sellers** (pay monthly for calls to copy) or
**course sellers** (pay once for videos, then nothing). Both leave the trader
alone with their own execution.

**MMFX's difference:** the complete loop — analysis, tools, education, community
— *plus* a feedback mechanism that reads their real trading results back to them.

> **Positioning line:** Most educators sell you information and disappear.
> MMFX gives you the method *and* reads your actual results back to you.

## Brand voice

- **Direct, plain, unhyped.** Slightly blunt. Honest about difficulty.
- **Never** guru energy, emoji-spam, or wealth-flex imagery
- Talks about **process, discipline and skill** — never riches
- Respects the reader's intelligence; most have already been burned
- Warm and premium visually: deep ink tones, a signal-orange accent, clean type
  (see `04-CREATIVE-ASSETS.md`)

**Tone test:** if it sounds like a trading guru on a rented yacht, it's wrong.
If it sounds like a professional who'd tell you a hard truth, it's right.
