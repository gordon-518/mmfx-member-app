## ⭐ AI Trading Assistant — the flagship
**LP:** `/lp/ai-trading-assistant` · *Members only · launched 14 Aug 2026*

Connects **read-only** to the trader's live MT4/MT5 account, imports every trade
automatically (no manual logging), then shows:
- Real performance — win rate, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, equity curve
- **"Leaks"** — the behaviours costing money (revenge trading, oversized losses,
  overtrading), **in dollars**, tied to the exact trades
- A **discipline score** against rules the trader sets themselves
- **"Don's read"** — an AI coach reviewing their trades against their own goals

**Hook:** *"You don't have a strategy problem. You have a 'nobody's keeping
score' problem."*

**Trust line (use it early):** read-only — it can never trade or withdraw, and
the password is never stored.

**Why it's the lead:** no competitor in this space offers it, and it names the
exact pain the audience feels — *"I don't know what I'm doing wrong."*

## The bigger story

Individual features are the hooks; this is the frame they sit in:

> Trading profitably isn't one skill — it's a loop:
> **Analysis → Execution → Trade Management**, on a foundation of education.

Everything maps to a stage: Daily Analysis and the Fundamental Desk for
**analysis**; indicators, strategies and signals for **execution**; the **AI
Trading Assistant** for **trade management** — the stage almost every retail
trader skips, and the reason most never improve.

**That's the pitch:** most educators sell information and disappear. MMFX gives
the method *and* reads your actual results back to you.
