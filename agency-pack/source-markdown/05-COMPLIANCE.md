# 05 · Compliance — read before writing a single ad

> **These are not stylistic preferences.** MMFX operates in a regulated space
> (retail forex/CFD education and introducing-broker referral) and advertises on
> a platform that treats financial services as a restricted category. A careless
> line can get an ad rejected, an ad account restricted, or create a regulatory
> problem for the client.
>
> **When in doubt, ask Don. Never guess.**

---

## 1. Platform risk — verify before you build

Forex/CFD-adjacent advertising sits under Meta's **financial products and
services** policies. Depending on the market, complex financial products can
require **prior written permission or advertiser authorisation**, and some
jurisdictions restrict the category outright.

**We are asking you, as the specialists, to confirm before campaign build:**

1. Is the ad account **in good standing** and eligible to advertise financial
   education / trading products in each target market?
2. Does any target market require **advertiser verification or written
   permission** for this category?
3. Are there **market-specific restrictions** that rule a geo in or out?
4. What's the current policy position on **trading education vs. financial
   advice** — we are firmly education/tools, not advice, and creative must make
   that obvious.

Platform policy changes frequently. **Please verify against current published
policy rather than relying on this document.**

**If an ad is rejected or the account is flagged, tell Don immediately.** In a
restricted category, repeated rejections escalate fast.

## 2. ❌ Never say, imply, or show

### Earnings and results — the hardest rule
- ❌ Any **profit, income or return** — guaranteed, typical, projected or implied
- ❌ "Make $X a day/week/month", "replace your salary", "passive income"
- ❌ **Profit screenshots** — account balances, P&L, broker statements, win streaks
- ❌ Win rates or accuracy claims ("80% accurate signals")
- ❌ Testimonials that reference money made
- ❌ "Risk-free", "guaranteed", "can't lose", "sure thing"
- ❌ Backtest or historical performance figures

### Advice and outcome
- ❌ Anything that reads as **financial advice** or a personal recommendation
- ❌ "We'll make you profitable" / "turn your account around"
- ❌ Claiming any tool **predicts** the market
- ❌ Suggesting trading is easy, quick, or low-risk

### Pressure and imagery
- ❌ Urgency that pushes someone to deposit money ("last chance", countdowning a
  broker deposit)
- ❌ Wealth-flex imagery — cars, watches, cash, private jets, mansions
- ❌ Framing the broker deposit as a payment to MMFX (it isn't — it's the
  member's own capital in their own account)
- ❌ Targeting or implying targeting of vulnerable or financially distressed people

## 3. ✅ Safe and true

- ✅ MMFX provides **education, analysis and tools** — not advice
- ✅ The 7-day trial is **genuinely free**, no card required
- ✅ The AI Trading Assistant is **read-only** — it can never trade or withdraw,
  and the password is never stored
- ✅ Specific, factual product claims: *19 lessons*, *10 indicators*, *4 eBooks*,
  *published every session*
- ✅ **Trading carries risk of loss** — include risk messaging where required
- ✅ Outcomes depend entirely on the trader's own decisions
- ✅ Past performance never indicates future results

## 4. Safe vs unsafe — rewrite table

| ❌ Unsafe | ✅ Safe |
|---|---|
| "Stop losing money" | "See where your money is leaking" |
| "Make consistent profits" | "Build a consistent process" |
| "Our signals are 80% accurate" | "Trade ideas from the desk, with the reasoning" |
| "This indicator predicts reversals" | "This indicator highlights potential reversal zones" |
| "Turn your trading around" | "See what your account is actually doing" |
| "Trade like a pro and earn like one" | "Learn the system a professional actually uses" |
| "Free $500 account!" | "The desk is free — you just need a funded account to trade with" |
| "Join 10,000 profitable traders" | "Join traders learning the MM system" |

**The pattern:** describe **what the product does**, never **what the trader
will earn**.

## 5. Feature-specific cautions

| Feature | Watch out for |
|---|---|
| **Signals** ⚠️ | Our most sensitive surface. Never quote accuracy, win rate, pips or profit. Signals are *trade ideas to evaluate*, never advice or an income source. |
| **Strategies** | They're backtestable — but **never publish backtest returns** or imply future performance. |
| **Indicators** | Analysis tools that highlight conditions. They do not predict. |
| **AI Trading Assistant** | Lead with **read-only / can't trade / password never stored**. It analyses; it doesn't advise or execute. |
| **Course** | Teaches a method. It doesn't promise an outcome. |
| **The IB offer** | The deposit is the member's own capital in their own name — never "pay us". Disclose the broker relationship where required. |

## 6. Using member content

- **Any** testimonial, review, screenshot or named member requires **written
  consent** — ask Don, don't source from Telegram or social
- No member's results may be presented as typical or expected
- No aggregate stats (member counts, success rates) without Don confirming the
  figure and that it's safe to publish

## 7. Sign-off

**Every concept goes to Don for approval before it runs** — copy and visual.

Fastest path to approval:
1. Check it against §2 (the never list)
2. Run it through the §4 rewrite table
3. Ask: *does this promise an outcome, or describe a product?* Only the second
   passes
